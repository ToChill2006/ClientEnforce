## Post 2 — "Required vs optional" (insight / lesson)

The single biggest mistake I see in client onboarding:

Everything is "optional."

The engagement letter. The ID. The source-of-funds declaration. The signed brief. All technically required — but in practice, sent as a polite email attachment and hoped for.

Then the work starts anyway, because nobody wants to hold up the project.

Six weeks in, you realise half the file is missing and you're the one reconstructing it.

The fix isn't a better email template. It's enforcement.

Required = the client literally can't mark onboarding complete until it's in.
Optional = nice to have, won't block.

That one distinction changes how the whole engagement runs.

It's the reason ClientEnforce exists, and it's the first thing I'd tell any firm to fix — whether you use our tool or not.
